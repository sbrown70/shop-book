# 1966 Mustang — Quick Reference

Handy baseline facts for the 289 / C4 car. Verify anything critical against a 1966 Mustang / 289 shop manual for your exact car and options.

## Drivetrain
- **Engine:** 289 cu in (4.7 L) Windsor V8
- **Transmission:** C4 three-speed automatic ("Cruise-O-Matic"); transmission mounts at the rear crossmember; torque converter attaches to the flexplate with (typically) **4** nuts/bolts
- **Flexplate/converter:** engine has a flexplate (not a flywheel); the torque converter stays with the transmission

## Electrical
- **System:** 12-volt, **negative ground**
- **Charging:** alternator (Ford moved to alternators in 1965)
- **Ignition:** points-and-condenser distributor, front-mounted

## Engine details (289)
- **Firing order:** 1-5-4-2-6-3-7-8
- **Cylinder numbering:** passenger (right) bank 1-2-3-4 front-to-back; driver (left) bank 5-6-7-8
- **Rear main seal:** two-piece neoprene lip seal, split at the crank centerline (upper half in block, lower half in rear main cap)
- **Oil capacity:** ~5 qts with a new filter
- **Approx. dressed engine weight:** ~460–575 lb (well within a 2-ton hoist)

## Reference torque values (verify before final assembly)
- Bellhousing-to-block bolts: ~40–45 ft-lb
- Torque converter-to-flexplate: ~20–25 ft-lb
- Motor mount through-bolts: ~45–55 ft-lb
- Rear main cap and other internal fasteners: look up exact spec — don't guess on bearing caps

## Factory options that change the engine-pull steps
- **Power steering** (Ford control-valve/pump system) — extra pump + lines to move aside
- **A/C** — compressor to unbolt and hang aside; **do not open the refrigerant system**
- Confirm which, if any, this car has before starting.
